# Purpose of This Document

This document provides enduring project context for humans and AI systems.

It serves as the constitutional document for the project and defines:

- Why the project exists
- What the project aims to achieve
- How decisions should be made
- How humans and AI collaborate
- The principles that guide execution

Unlike `current-state.md`, which reflects operational status, this document captures long-lived project intent and should change infrequently.

---

# Project Overview

Wild Free Gallery is an AI-native product initiative, portfolio, and public case study documenting the journey from concept to execution.

The project is intentionally developed as:

1. A real digital product and creative business.
2. A living demonstration of modern Product Management.
3. A demonstration of AI-native Product Leadership.
4. A public record of product discovery, design, engineering, communication, and continuous learning.

The project exists to demonstrate what thoughtful human leadership combined with effective AI collaboration can accomplish.

---

# Vision

Create a world-class demonstration of AI-native Product Leadership through the conception, design, engineering, launch, operation, communication, and continuous evolution of a real product.

The project should inspire confidence through evidence rather than claims.

Visitors should be able to independently conclude the quality of the work by reviewing the artifacts, decisions, process, and outcomes.

---

# Mission

Build, launch, operate, and continuously improve Wild Free Gallery while openly documenting the product journey through high-quality artifacts, reviews, audits, and public learning.

---

# Why This Project Exists

Wild Free Gallery serves multiple purposes simultaneously:

- Build a real product.
- Create a meaningful creative business.
- Demonstrate Product Management capability.
- Demonstrate Product Leadership capability.
- Demonstrate Product Engineering capability.
- Demonstrate AI-native workflows.
- Demonstrate effective human-AI collaboration.
- Establish credibility through execution rather than self-promotion.

The project values demonstrated competence over stated expertise.

# Guiding Principles

## Human-Led AI

Humans retain responsibility for:

- Vision
- Strategy
- Prioritization
- Product Judgment
- Ethics
- Final Decisions

AI exists to accelerate execution, learning, exploration, and communication.

---

## Momentum Is A Feature

Progress matters.

Prefer shipping, learning, and iterating over excessive planning.

Make reversible decisions quickly.

Investigate irreversible decisions carefully.

---

## Demonstrate Through Artifacts

Claims should be supported by evidence.

Whenever possible, ideas should be represented through:

- Documents
- Decisions
- Prototypes
- Designs
- Code
- Reviews
- Audits

Artifacts are preferred over assertions.

---

## Continuous Validation

Regularly seek evidence that assumptions remain valid.

Use:

- Reviews
- Audits
- User feedback
- Product feedback
- Technical feedback

to guide future decisions.

---

## Product Craftsmanship

Pursue quality without pursuing perfection.

Favor:

- Clarity
- Simplicity
- Maintainability
- Thoughtfulness
- Strong execution

---

## Build In Public

Whenever practical and safe:

- Share progress
- Share learnings
- Share decisions
- Share outcomes

Private information, credentials, secrets, and sensitive business information should remain private.

Transparency should increase trust and learning.

---

## AI As Atmosphere, Not Interruption

AI should enhance experiences naturally.

The presence of AI should feel purposeful and supportive rather than distracting or performative.

---

## Continuous Learning

The project is expected to evolve.

New information should improve decisions.

Changing direction in response to evidence is a strength rather than a failure.

---

# Product Strategy

Wild Free Gallery should be experienced as a real product rather than a traditional portfolio.

The portfolio value emerges from:

- The work
- The decisions
- The process
- The documentation
- The reviews
- The outcomes

Visitors should feel they are observing a genuine product journey.

---

# Audit & Review Philosophy

Reviews and audits are first-class deliverables.

Major milestones should produce:

- Deliverables
- Reviews
- Lessons Learned
- Recommendations

Audits serve two purposes:

1. Improve project quality.
2. Demonstrate thoughtful product leadership.

The project should maintain an observable record of learning and improvement over time.

---

# Documentation Philosophy

Documentation is part of the product.

Documentation should:

- Support decision-making
- Improve onboarding
- Enable AI collaboration
- Improve maintainability
- Preserve institutional knowledge
- Demonstrate product thinking

Documentation should remain concise, useful, and actionable.

---

# Agent-First Operating Model

Project artifacts should be understandable by both humans and AI systems.

A future contributor should be able to understand:

- What the project is
- Why it exists
- What has been decided
- What is currently being worked on

without requiring access to historical conversations.

---

# Decision-Making Framework

When evaluating alternatives:

1. Does this create value?
2. Does this improve learning?
3. Can it be implemented simply?
4. Is it maintainable?
5. Does it support the project's mission?

When uncertainty is high, prefer experimentation and validation.

---

# Quality Standards

Artifacts should be:

- Clear
- Actionable
- Traceable
- Reusable
- Concise

Implementations should be:

- Maintainable
- Understandable
- Well-structured
- Incrementally extensible

---

# Long-Term Intent

Wild Free Gallery is intended to become a reusable reference implementation for future AI-native product initiatives.

The operating model, workflows, documentation standards, audit processes, and human-AI collaboration patterns developed here should be reusable across future products and ventures.
