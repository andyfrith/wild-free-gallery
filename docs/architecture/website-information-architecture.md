# Executive Summary

Wild Free Gallery is not a traditional portfolio website.

It is a living product ecosystem that combines:

- A real creative business
- A public product case study
- A build-in-public initiative
- An AI-native product leadership demonstration

The architecture should guide visitors through a progression of understanding.

## Within 30 Seconds

Visitors should understand:

- What Wild Free Gallery is
- That a real product is being built
- That progress is documented publicly
- That AI plays a meaningful role in the process

## Within 2 Minutes

Visitors should understand:

- Why the project exists
- What is being built
- What has already been accomplished
- Where evidence can be found

## Within 10 Minutes

Visitors should understand:

- Product strategy
- Decision-making approach
- Engineering and design philosophy
- Human-AI collaboration model
- The credibility of the work being presented

The experience should feel:

- Transparent
- Intentional
- Curious
- Evidence-driven
- Human
- Actively evolving

Visitors should feel as though they are exploring a working product studio rather than reviewing a traditional portfolio.

---

# Information Architecture Principles

## Clarity Over Complexity

Every page should answer a clear visitor question.

Avoid unnecessary navigation depth and duplicate content.

---

## Narrative Over Self-Promotion

Tell the story of the work.

Allow visitors to draw their own conclusions.

---

## Evidence Over Claims

Artifacts, implementations, decisions, audits, and outcomes are the primary proof points.

The architecture should elevate evidence above personal branding.

---

## Progressive Disclosure

Provide increasing levels of detail as visitor interest increases.

Support both quick review and deep exploration.

---

## Fast Exploration

Visitors should find meaningful content within seconds.

No important content should require extensive navigation.

---

## Build In Public

The architecture should expose:

- Progress
- Learnings
- Decisions
- Reviews
- Outcomes

over time.

---

## Living Documentation

The website should communicate that the project is active, evolving, and continuously improving.

---

# IA Implementation Principle

The website should be optimized around three visitor questions.

## Question 1

### What is being built?

Primary answers:

- Home
- Story

---

## Question 2

### How is it being built?

Primary answers:

- Journey
- AI Systems
- Audits

---

## Question 3

### Can I inspect the evidence?

Primary answers:

- Artifacts
- GitHub

---

Every page, component, navigation element, and future enhancement should support at least one of these three questions.

If a proposed feature does not improve understanding of one of these questions, it should be considered out of scope for the MVP.

---

# Creative Layer

Wild Free Gallery is ultimately a creative endeavor.

The gallery, artwork, artist perspective, and evolving creative process provide the narrative backdrop through which product leadership, engineering, design, and AI collaboration are demonstrated.

The creative identity should be present throughout the experience without overwhelming the primary product narrative.

Bizarro and the creative world of Wild Free Gallery should remain discoverable and evolve naturally over time.

---

# AI Systems Layer

AI is not a standalone feature of Wild Free Gallery.

AI is part of the operating environment of the project.

Throughout the website, visitors should be able to understand:

- How AI is used
- Where AI contributes
- Where humans lead
- What workflows exist
- What lessons have been learned

AI should appear as a natural part of the project's story rather than as a separate marketing narrative.

# Reviews & Audits Experience

Reviews and audits are first-class project artifacts.

## Timeline Model

Phase → Audit → Learning → Action

Every significant milestone should eventually produce an audit.

---

## Review Summary

Each audit should answer:

- What happened?
- What worked?
- What failed?
- What changed?
- What happens next?

---

## Learning Loop

Review → Insight → Decision → Action → Review

This loop should remain visible throughout the project.

---

## Continuous Improvement Narrative

Visitors should see evidence that the project evolves through learning rather than rigid planning.

## Architectural Goal

A visitor should be able to:

- Understand the project within 30 seconds.
- Discover meaningful evidence within 2 minutes.
- Develop confidence in the work within 10 minutes.

The architecture succeeds when visitors conclude:

- A real product is being built.
- A real business is being created.
- Product leadership is visible.
- Engineering capability is credible.
- AI is integrated thoughtfully.
- Progress is actively occurring.

These conclusions should emerge through evidence, narrative, transparency, and execution rather than self-promotion.
