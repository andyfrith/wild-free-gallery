# Executive Summary

Wild Free Gallery V1 is the smallest launchable version of a creative business and public product case study designed to demonstrate AI-native Product Leadership through execution rather than claims.

The MVP combines a curated public website, product artifacts, documented decisions, and implementation evidence into a cohesive experience that allows visitors to independently evaluate product thinking, design quality, engineering capability, and effective human-AI collaboration.

The objective is not feature completeness.

The objective is credibility through execution.

V1 should be launchable within approximately one week and serve as the foundation for future product evolution.

---

# Problem Statement

## What Problem Does The MVP Solve?

Professionals increasingly claim expertise in Product Management, AI, Product Strategy, Design, Engineering, and Innovation.

However, it is often difficult for employers, founders, and collaborators to evaluate these claims through tangible evidence.

Most portfolios focus on outcomes without revealing the process, decisions, tradeoffs, and execution behind them.

Wild Free Gallery addresses this gap by creating a public environment where visitors can inspect the actual work:

- Product artifacts
- Decisions
- Documentation
- Engineering implementations
- Reviews
- Audits
- Learnings

Rather than asking visitors to trust assertions, the product allows them to evaluate evidence.

## Why Should It Exist?

The project exists to demonstrate what AI-native Product Leadership looks like when applied to a real business initiative.

It provides:

- Transparency
- Practical examples
- Learning opportunities
- Evidence of execution
- Public accountability

# Risks

## Scope Creep

Risk:

Attempting to build too much.

Mitigation:

Strict adherence to MVP scope.

---

## Over-Planning

Risk:

Documentation delaying delivery.

Mitigation:

Prioritize shipping.

---

## Documentation Outpacing Implementation

Risk:

Artifacts become more complete than product.

Mitigation:

Maintain implementation momentum.

---

## Technical Complexity

Risk:

Architecture exceeds MVP needs.

Mitigation:

Favor simple solutions.

---

## Narrative Confusion

Risk:

Visitors misunderstand project purpose.

Mitigation:

Maintain consistent messaging.

# Assumptions

The MVP assumes:

- One-week launch remains feasible.
- Single repository approach remains appropriate.
- Documentation remains a strategic asset.
- Build-in-public remains acceptable.
- AI-assisted workflows continue throughout development.
- Initial audience values transparency and artifacts.

### Core Outcome

A visitor should be able to arrive at the site, spend fifteen minutes exploring, and independently conclude that:

- A real product exists.
- A real business is being built.
- Product thinking is visible.
- Engineering execution is credible.
- AI is being used intentionally.
- Progress is actively occurring.

If those conclusions can be reached without additional explanation, the V1 MVP has succeeded.
