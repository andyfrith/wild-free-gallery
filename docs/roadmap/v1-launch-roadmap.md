# Executive Summary

This roadmap defines the path from project conception to the first public release of Wild Free Gallery.

The objective is not feature completeness.

The objective is to launch a polished, credible, and publicly reviewable MVP that demonstrates:

- AI-native Product Leadership
- Product Strategy
- Product Design
- Product Engineering
- Human-AI Collaboration
- Build-in-Public Execution

Target launch timeline:

**One Week MVP**

# MVP Success Definition

The MVP succeeds when a visitor can:

- Understand the project within 30 seconds
- Discover meaningful evidence within 2 minutes
- Develop confidence in the work within 10 minutes

The MVP does not require:

- Revenue
- Advanced AI features
- Large traffic volumes
- Marketplace functionality

The MVP requires:

- Strong storytelling
- Clear documentation
- Professional design
- Credible implementation
- Public accessibility

# Roadmap Philosophy

Wild Free Gallery is intentionally iterative.

The roadmap should evolve as new information is discovered.

Changes are expected.

The roadmap exists to maintain direction while enabling learning.

Success is measured by progress, execution quality, and continuous improvement rather than strict adherence to an original plan.
